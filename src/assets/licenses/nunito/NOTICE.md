# Nunito UI

Nunito variable font, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/nunito
The complete upstream Nunito[wght].ttf was converted to WOFF2 without removing characters. The bundled font includes Vietnamese glyphs and supports weights 200 through 1000.
